Composer, Producer & Sound Designer

I'm a composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist (piano and guitar) with 7 years of independent production rooted in the Plugg scene. I'm completing a Music Theory & Composition certificate at Berklee College of Music, which informs everything I make - from formal harmonic choices to the way I structure a piece to land emotionally. I score games and film, design sounds and presets, produce for artists, and create sample packs - all filtered through a sonic identity that's ethereal, atmospheric, and emotionally direct.

My strongest skill is writing beautiful, emotionally resonant melodies quickly and instinctively, then building full instruments around them and shaping them into full pieces that feel melodic, playful, catchy, harmonically rich, and emotionally deep. I love layering in ear candy - the small details and textures that keep pulling listeners back in. A lot of my work sits slightly outside the mainstream. I'm interested in intentional artistic choices, whether that means unexpected harmony, unusual textures, or distorted elements used in a creative way. The result is music that feels niche, expressive, and true to itself.

I bring formal composition training, instrumental skill across multiple instruments, 7 years of underground credibility with an established US collaboration network, and a sonic identity you can hear across everything I touch. My work has proven it connects widely - one of my productions was featured in The FADER's Top 30 Rap Songs of 2024, and another has reached over 5 million streams. I also have 250+ hours of streamed producer-method content.

If you're a game developer, film director, artist, or label - someone who values originality and emotional impact - I'd love to hear what you're building. Send me a reference, concept, vocal, or rough idea as your inspiration, and I can create something custom around your vision.

Hit the Contact button above and let's make something special.

If you want something safe and generic, I may not be the right fit. But if you want a beat that feels original, expressive, and memorable, we'll probably work well together.

I'm also continually sharpening my craft and am currently studying part-time online through Berklee, which helps me keep developing both technically and creatively.

My work has already connected on a larger scale as well - one of my beats was featured on The FADER's Top 30 Rap Songs of 2024, and another track I produced grew to over 5 million streams.

You can send me a reference, a vocal, a rough idea, or even just a mood or inspiration, and I can build something custom around it. I'd especially love to work with artists who care about originality and emotional impact.

Hit the Contact button above and let's make something special.
